Upload a yi-hack tgz package (e.g. yi_dome_0.4.3b.tgz). After the upload completes, press Install: the camera backs up its configuration, installs the package and reboots. The package installs the yi-hack tree and, when the SD card is FAT32, also updates the camera's baseline (rootfs and home partitions).
